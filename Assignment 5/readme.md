**Category:** Cybersecurity — Information Gathering & Reconnaissance

## Objective

To perform basic reconnaissance and information gathering against the assigned lab target, identify relevant services and open ports, and answer the associated lab questions.

## Methodology

1. **Target identification:** Connected to the assigned lab environment and identified the target system.
2. **Port scanning:** Used Nmap to identify open ports and accessible services.
3. **Service enumeration:** Examined the discovered services and their versions.
4. **Domain information lookup:** Checked the available domain registration information.
5. **Answer verification:** Submitted the findings in the lab and verified the answers.

## Key Findings

- Identified **7 open ports** on the assigned target.
- Identified **SSH** running on port `22`.
- Identified **HTTP** on port `80`.
- Identified the FTP service as `vsftpd 3.0.5` on port `21`.
- Recorded the domain registration date as `22/11/2019`.
- Verified the answers through the lab platform.
